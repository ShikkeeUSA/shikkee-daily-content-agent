## Shikkee angle (one angle, all channels)
Bezos is right about the diagnosis and stops one step short of the cure.

He is correct that it is supply and demand. But "build more supply" is still an input problem. It tells cities to approve more and builders to pour more foundations, then leaves the buyer standing on a lot watching the price float until the day they get keys. More supply of the old product just means more homes whose final number nobody can promise you at the start.

Shikkee attacks the part Bezos did not name: the home itself is still a construction project, not a product. We sell the home as hardware plus an OS. Fixed price, locked at reservation. 30-day assembly. So affordability is not a forecast you hope for after supply loosens up, it is a number on the page the day you order.

Permitting still applies to every Shikkee home, same as any home. That part does not change and we do not pretend it does. What we fixed is everything after it: the price does not move and the build does not drag, no matter how your local market behaves.

Econ 101 explains why housing got expensive. Shikkee is the product that makes the price stop moving.
